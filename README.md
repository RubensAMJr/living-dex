# Living Dex Checklist

A one-page Pokémon living dex tracker: all 1,025 species in National Dex order plus Alolan, Galarian, Hisuian and Paldean forms, with a separate shiny dex.

- Tap or click a Pokémon to mark it caught; progress is saved in your browser (per device).
- Search by name, number or region; filter by generation, region forms, caught or missing.
- The ✨ button switches to the shiny dex. Menu → Export/Import backup moves your progress between devices.

Artwork is loaded from Bulbapedia, Serebii and an imgbb gallery at view time; nothing is stored in this repository.
